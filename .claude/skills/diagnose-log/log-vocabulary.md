# The named log lines, and what each measures

A starting glossary, not a complete one: `grep -n '<LABEL>' server.js` finds the exact string and the comment above it. Grouped by stage. Round pointers say where the line was introduced or last corrected.

## Find and contact
`DISCOVERY START` · `FIND JOB` ([§93](../../../docs/history/round-093.md)) · `LEAD BENCH` (banked/served; a refused write here was silent for weeks, [§93](../../../docs/history/round-093.md)) · `DEPTH YIELD` (what bought pages returned, [§12](../../../docs/history/round-012.md)) · `📉 FIND YIELD` (what the bench contributed vs what the budget assumed, [§94](../../../docs/history/round-094.md)) · `GOOGLE PLACES` (every billed call named, [§84](../../../docs/history/round-084.md)) · `FIND READ` (real page paths, [§104](../../../docs/history/round-104.md)) · `ROSTER [` (what the team-page parser paired; the hint sentence now searches every vocabulary, [§99](../../../docs/history/round-099.md)) · `DM [` / `DM/bizname` / `DM/websearch` (owner sources) · `💸 OWNER WAVE` (paid wave bought? produced? cost — the free-settle rate, [§100](../../../docs/history/round-100.md), [§104](../../../docs/history/round-104.md)) · `📇 FIND CONTACT` (per-lead result, credits, dollars) · `🔗 CHAIN OUTLETS` ([§24](../../../docs/history/round-024.md), [§97](../../../docs/history/round-097.md)) · `LISTING RECOVERED` ([§104](../../../docs/history/round-104.md)) · `🌐 WEBSITE RESOLVE` ([§105](../../../docs/history/round-105.md)) · `EMAIL RESULT` (address, tier, sendable) · `SMTP-verified` / `Catch-all probe` (the verifier works; some hosts stall RCPT TO by design, [§3](../../../docs/history/round-003.md)) · `🔴 EMAIL VERIFIER OUT OF CREDITS` / `🟢 EMAIL VERIFIER: back` ([§94](../../../docs/history/round-094.md), [§95](../../../docs/history/round-095.md)).

## Research
`FC PAID [map|scrape|search]` (each Firecrawl call by kind, [§76](../../../docs/history/round-076.md)) · `FIRECRAWL SPEND` (credits per lead; renders have their own bucket, [§81](../../../docs/history/round-081.md)) · `FIRECRAWL RATE LIMITED` / `THROTTLED Nx` (a 429 holds the whole gate, [§39](../../../docs/history/round-039.md)) · `FIRECRAWL PACE` (per-endpoint pacing learned from their header, [§50](../../../docs/history/round-050.md)) · `FIRECRAWL OUT OF CREDITS` (the latch; it re-tests after a cooldown, [§43](../../../docs/history/round-043.md)) · `REVIEW MINE` / `DEEP PAIN: N verified repeating patterns` ([§5](../../../docs/history/round-005.md), [§54](../../../docs/history/round-054.md)) · `RANK ROW` / `LOCAL PACK [` / `LOCAL RANK` / `RANK SOURCE` / `SHEET RANK` (which source answered, where the searcher stood, the row the sheet renders, [§52](../../../docs/history/round-052.md), [§76](../../../docs/history/round-076.md), [§78](../../../docs/history/round-078.md), [§82](../../../docs/history/round-082.md), [§85](../../../docs/history/round-085.md)) · `DFS AUTH PROBE` (no line at all = credentials never reached the instance, [§59](../../../docs/history/round-059.md), [§64](../../../docs/history/round-064.md)) · `LISTING`/`Place Details` · `⏱ TIME` (seconds per service per lead, and gate wait — decides whether the throttle is ours, [§24](../../../docs/history/round-024.md), [§50](../../../docs/history/round-050.md)) · `JOB … done in Xs of WORK` (work vs queue, [§24](../../../docs/history/round-024.md), [§51](../../../docs/history/round-051.md)) · `HOLDING` (the memory admission gate; a flat 90 s hold means the ceiling is below the process weight, [§50](../../../docs/history/round-050.md)).

## Audit and email
`BRAIN INPUT` / `BRAIN COST` / `💰 ANTHROPIC TOTAL` (every model call named, sorted by cost; `cacheRead=0` on a first call is not a broken cache, [§9](../../../docs/history/round-009.md), [§54](../../../docs/history/round-054.md)) · `BRAIN GATE` (the audit is a husk; now decided before the story and fact-check are bought, [§44](../../../docs/history/round-044.md), [§83](../../../docs/history/round-083.md)) · `harm ladder failed — X is not defined` (the whole ladder died; every lead reads as clean, [§40](../../../docs/history/round-040.md)) · `COMPOSE TRACE` · `LADDER OVERRIDE` / `INTERNAL ONLY` / `SELF-FIXABLE, NOT LEADING` (why a finding did not lead, [§2](../../../docs/history/round-002.md), [§24](../../../docs/history/round-024.md)) · `SITUATION READ` / `↺ SITUATION READ` (the story writer; the retry is the most expensive line on the lead, [§67](../../../docs/history/round-067.md), [§81](../../../docs/history/round-081.md)) · `SITUATION READ GATED` (which stripper removed which sentence, [§84](../../../docs/history/round-084.md)) · `⛔ FACT CHECK` / `FACT CHECK DID NOT RUN` (the last gate before a prospect; a parse failure here used to be our own quoted spine, [§39](../../../docs/history/round-039.md), [§45](../../../docs/history/round-045.md)) · `⛔ INVENTED MONEY` / `AUDIT MONEY` ([§24](../../../docs/history/round-024.md), [§44](../../../docs/history/round-044.md)) · `⛔ CLAIM VERIFY` (a scope note, not an assertion, [§24](../../../docs/history/round-024.md)) · `SOURCE VERIFY` / `AUDIT UNIQUENESS` · `CANDIDATE HYGIENE` ([§71](../../../docs/history/round-071.md)).
