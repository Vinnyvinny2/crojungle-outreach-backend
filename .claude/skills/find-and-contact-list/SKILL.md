# Find and the contact list — the stage in use today

**Goal:** After reading this, Claude can explain what a Find press and a contact read do to a lead, read the log lines they print, and tell a confirmed owner, email or phone from a guess.

New text (2026-09-02) distilled from rounds 89–105; **every sentence cites the round it came from**, so any line can be checked by opening that one file. The CSV columns in `csv-columns.md` beside this file are generated from the app's own table. Nothing here changes a mechanism; it describes the ones that ship. What the rep receives is a LIST, not an audit: the Research tab audits, the Find tab lists ([§90](../../../docs/history/round-090.md)).

## 1. A Find press — what it buys and what it keeps

- One press is one background job: the browser submits, then polls, because a real press takes 100–120 seconds and the request used to be cut at 60 ([§93](../../../docs/history/round-093.md)). One run at a time; a second press returns the running job. Up to `FIND_RUN_MAX` (default 300) leads come back per press; the queue holds up to 1,000 ([§93](../../../docs/history/round-093.md), [§94](../../../docs/history/round-094.md)).
- It searches the declared trades (`GP_CATEGORIES`, 55 as of [§94](../../../docs/history/round-094.md)) across the declared metros (`GP_CITIES`, 23) on Google Places, about 60 searches a press = **$2.10**, inside Google's free 1,000 calls a month ([§100](../../../docs/history/round-100.md)). Google bills per CALL, not per business, so filtering harder never saves money; only fewer calls do ([§12](../../../docs/history/round-012.md)).
- Two memories keep it from re-buying the same ground: a category + city pair that returns nothing new twice rests for 30 days ([§11](../../../docs/history/round-011.md)), and every qualified lead the press did not return goes on the **bench** and is served next press before anything is spent ([§12](../../../docs/history/round-012.md)). Both need their Supabase tables writable — row-level security silently refused every bench write for weeks, which made every demotion a deletion ([§93](../../../docs/history/round-093.md)).
- What is DELETED at discovery: named franchises (`GP_FRANCHISE`, entries qualified so "rainbow" no longer deletes Rainbow Roofing, [§96](../../../docs/history/round-096.md)), chains seen in three or more metros in one run — with the bench as its memory across runs ([§24](../../../docs/history/round-024.md), [§97](../../../docs/history/round-097.md)), brand names matched as a WHOLE name only ([§94](../../../docs/history/round-094.md)), institutions and scale words ([§14](../../../docs/history/round-014.md)), and businesses over the size gate — where a verified headcount overrules any name pattern ([§14](../../../docs/history/round-014.md)).
- What is DEMOTED, never deleted: a rating above 4.85 (no negative reviews left to mine; there is no floor any more — a low rating is pain, [§94](../../../docs/history/round-094.md)), a review count above 2,000 (750 until [§111](../../../docs/history/round-111.md); [§17](../../../docs/history/round-017.md)), and a per-category cap ([§13](../../../docs/history/round-013.md)). Demotion now lowers the printed score ([§93](../../../docs/history/round-093.md)).
- The review FLOOR is a job count, raised for high-volume trades (pest control) and lowered to 5 for low-volume high-ticket ones (custom builders, CPAs), because the richest leads in the ICP have nine reviews ([§94](../../../docs/history/round-094.md)).
- The non-Places lanes (job boards, funding, news, for-sale) are OFF by default: they carry no Google listing, so none of the ICP rules can judge them ([§96](../../../docs/history/round-096.md)). TheirStack is the one worth funding — a marketing job posting is the only clock in the pipeline ([§104](../../../docs/history/round-104.md)).
- Two numbers on a card: the **Find score** (what we thought before reading the site) and, after a read, the **FIT score** (what we found). The **affordability band** — premium fit / lower tier only / below our floor — comes from the trade's job value times the jobs on record, capped for one-man trades, sharpened by a published team and published hours; it never produces a dollar figure because revenue is not measured ([§94](../../../docs/history/round-094.md), [§96](../../../docs/history/round-096.md)).

## 2. A contact read — what it does to one lead

- "Get contacts for N" reads N leads through a pool of six; a lead REFUSED before spending does not use a slot, so five asked means five read ([§102](../../../docs/history/round-102.md), [§104](../../../docs/history/round-104.md)). Three transport failures in a row stop the run (a dead server is one fact, not a hundred failed leads, [§90](../../../docs/history/round-090.md)); Stop aborts requests in flight ([§91](../../../docs/history/round-091.md)).
- **Refused at zero cost**, with a permanent Ruled-out verdict: a national franchise, a listed brand, an institution (`nameIsOutOfIcp`, [§97](../../../docs/history/round-097.md)). A lead with a name and no website (news / SEC lanes) first gets a website resolved from free sources — two sources naming the same host, judged by one predicate, ambiguity resolves nothing — or is refused before a slot is taken; a resolved site is confirmed against the page or un-stamped, and the listing recovery cannot run on an unconfirmed domain ([§105](../../../docs/history/round-105.md)).
- **The free read is the door; Firecrawl is the fallback.** A plain fetch of the homepage and the pages its own navigation names — team and about first, careers last, up to twenty pages, content-deduplicated, 120 seconds for the whole read, stopping early once the owner is found ([§90](../../../docs/history/round-090.md), [§103](../../../docs/history/round-103.md)). Firecrawl is asked only when a site refuses the plain fetch, and then without a screenshot ([§90](../../../docs/history/round-090.md)). A lead with no Google listing gets one recovered from its confirmed domain, which unlocks the rating, hours, category and the review-reply owner source ([§104](../../../docs/history/round-104.md)).
- **Since [§110](../../../docs/history/round-110.md), two measurements the rep reads:** `Size` (low / medium / high, from verified or published headcount, fleet, locations and the metro count; age and reviews alone are a "guess" and say so) with a confidence word (sure / likely / guess / unknown); and `Target`, picked from the LAYERS, not the size: the owner when he is within reach (named on his pages, signing reviews, a personal mailbox, no org chart) at any size, the director-level marketing decision-maker (from their own roster, else Hunter's marketing filter, never a Firecrawl credit) when the business is layered. A Marketing Manager or Coordinator is never shown. The owner and email grade letters left the export; the card keeps them. Log line: `🎯 TARGET`.
- **Since [§111](../../../docs/history/round-111.md), the size is on one ladder and every lead is in a lane.** The rep's word is pinned to the tier: **low** = $800k–$1.2M, **medium** = $1.2M–$10M, **high** = $10M–$30M, from `ICP_REVENUE_BAND` with the head-to-dollar conversion per trade (six people are a core HVAC shop and an entry law firm). When nothing published a size the read buys ONE lookup (Companies API, then a snippet-only directory search, ~1 credit) and labels it a directory's — log line `📏 SIZE LOOKUP`. Then `lanesFor` decides: **call** (owner within reach, entry or above) goes to the rep's CSV and Google Sheet, which now carry the call lane and nothing else; **email** (core or above with a named owner or marketing head) is the Research lane; layered businesses and TheirStack leads are email only; an owner-run core lead is in both, call first. The card shows size, target and a lane chip (`CALL` / `EMAIL` / `CALL + EMAIL` / `TOO SMALL`). TheirStack is on by default when its key is set, asks for 6–150 staff hiring marketing in our 23 metros within 30 days, and `📉 FIND YIELD` counts its leads per tier.
- **Since [§112](../../../docs/history/round-112.md), the size is measured before it is guessed, and the Read tab has four buckets.** The lookup fires unless a headcount, fleet, location count, stated revenue or a team page at the core cut was read (tenure and reviews never count); on a miss it buys one more snippet-only query over LinkedIn company pages and BBB profiles; a directory's figure alone is a *guess*, *likely* with one agreeing fact, *sure* with two, and "<$5M" is a bucket, not $5M. The lane's fallback is the sheet's own review-count guess, so the two cannot disagree. **The call lane means a named owner**: a read lead with a phone and nobody named is **No name yet** (off the sheet, never deleted). The Read tab shows **Call list · Email lane · No name yet · Too small** with counts; the CSV and the Sheet are the Call list only; the Email lane's button moves its leads to Research; the Result line says where every read row went. The owner ladder names more people: the city parser reads "Phoenix AZ" and "Phoenix, Arizona" (and falls back on the market the lead was found in), the web search ends with a LinkedIn owner query, the OpenCorporates officer stage (behind `OPENCORPORATES_KEY`, a paid plan - no free tier since 2026-09-03) names the filed president or member and refuses agents, an eponymous owner can sign whatever the title under the photo says, and the name door refuses organisation tokens (a client logo beside a title is not a person).
- **Since [§113](../../../docs/history/round-113.md), a directory headcount is *likely* on its own, and a product company is an email lead.** The confidence rule (Vin: "yes, that rule"): a directory HEADCOUNT - exact, or a range that sits inside one tier - is *likely* alone; a range across tiers is a *guess*; a directory REVENUE alone stays a *guess*; each agreeing fact lifts one step; the review count no longer votes. The second size query is bought when the first PARSE found nothing, never on the wording of a snippet. One location (the address, else the market the press found the lead in) serves the owner ladder AND the size lookup, and the web-search negative memo is keyed on the city too. The name door refuses family / team / brothers tokens ("Larsen Family"); the chain tells read a state abbreviation under `/locations/` and a "Brand - City" listing whose own page is a per-location page. **A product company or manufacturer** (two product tells - a cart, free shipping, a best seller, wholesale, a dealer locator, "we manufacture", contact sales - and no service area) is kept and routed to the **Email lane only**, like a layered business: Vin, 2026-09-03, "keep them in, but these are likely email leads". And the rule for me: ask Vin before every merge and never merge while a batch runs - a docs-only merge restarted Render mid-batch and cost 6 reads and 17 unstarted leads.
- **Since [§114](../../../docs/history/round-114.md), the ceiling is a reachability rule, big companies are email leads, and the call sheet is hedged.** The call cap is **$35M** (headroom on a measured number; staff cut 175, trucks 117); an owner-run business over it is still called while its measured dollars (headcount x the trade's revenue per head, trucks x per truck, a stated revenue) sit under **$50M**; the email lane has **no ceiling**. A lead is benched under the $800k floor only when its size is *likely* or *sure* - a guess stays on the sheet as low. **Chain evidence carries a kind**: a franchise title or a page selling franchises DROPS the lead; per-city location pages, four state pages or a "Brand - City" outlet mark a **branch network**, kept for email. "Portfolio company" / "backed by X Capital" / "a division of" marks **PE-owned**, "serving nationwide" marks a **national operator**, both kept for email; only the franchisee disclosure drops (tested first, so a PE-backed franchisee is a franchisee). **The hedge** (Vin: *"it's better to never find out if we cut it"*): a layered business, a branch network, PE-owned or national UNDER the cap stays on the call sheet, **ranked last** (a -8 demotion on the FIT score), with "ask for the marketing head" in the lane note; over the cap they are email only. Only TheirStack leads (no phone) and product companies are never on the sheet. TheirStack asks up to 5,000 staff; a verified headcount past the cap is DEMOTED at discovery (`DEMOTED` in the log), never deleted; `GP_LARGE_SHARE` (default 0.1) serves a slice of each run from the size-demoted pool and `📉 FIND YIELD` counts it. Log: `🎯 TARGET` prints "branch network (…)", "PE-owned (…)", "national (…)" and a lane note that says "on the call sheet last". The Read tab's captions were fixed in [§115](../../../docs/history/round-115.md).
- **Since [§115](../../../docs/history/round-115.md) (the first Round 114 run, 25 leads, lanes reconciled):** a listing named **"<Brand> of <City>" whose own page is /<city> on the brand's domain** is a franchise territory and DROPS (Archadeck of Columbus; `archadeck` joined the name list); a **branch network is measured as the brand** (the SIZE LOOKUP line says "searched as …") and is **layered by construction** - "locally owned and operated" on a chain's location page no longer reads as owner-run, so the target is the marketing head or the owner is "expect not to answer"; a **product company is an email lead whatever the tier guess** (Mission Solar fell to entry on 37 reviews and got no lane); the page picker never fetches an asset (a .css file was read as a contact page); the size lookup's LinkedIn/BBB query is bought from 150 reviews up, or when the count is unknown (0 hits on 21 misses). Client (contract 20260930): the **website** column is back in the lean sheet (17 columns); the hedge's rows sort **last** on the screen, the CSV and the Sheet; a grade-D name prints **"Held back: …"** instead of "Owner:"; the fourth bucket is **"No lane"**.
- **Since [§116](../../../docs/history/round-116.md) (Vin: "get it to 8 tonight"), a retired person never reaches the sheet and three free size sources run.** A name the page, the roster, the search results or a title says is **retired** (retired, emeritus, in memoriam, the late, "(ret.)", former owner, sold the business - in the same sentence as the full name; a retired firefighter who owns the shop is not retired from it) is refused at every source (`DM/brain … is retired per their own page`, `DM/websearch … retired per the results - REJECTED`, `DM/door … is retired - refused`, `ROSTER … skipped - retired`) and the ladder continues. Sizes: the **team page is counted without titles** (name-shaped headings, testimonials excluded, from three up, a floor); **their own words are read wider** ("team of twelve", "15-person team", "employs over 40", "three crews" at about three a crew, "12-truck fleet"; never years, stars, hours, generations or percentages); and the **BBB profile is fetched free** off a URL the size search already returned (a plain fetch, never a credit; a refusal is one log line) for "Number of Employees" (a directory headcount, *likely*), "Business Started" and "Business Management", whose named manager ships as the owner graded C when nobody else named anyone (`DM/bbb`). The Result line prints **"Sizes: N measured (x%) - N sure, N likely; N a guess, N not measured"**, which is the score the two proof runs are judged on: 0 retired / franchise / chain as Owner, sizes measured ≥ 50%, named ≥ 80%.
- **Since [§117](../../../docs/history/round-117.md) (Vin: "short stuff in the rows") the sheet cell is a name and a three-word flag, and a branch is caught before the money is spent.** The "Who to go to" cell reads `Owner: Matt Bauer`, `Owner: Joe Novogratz - ask marketing head`, `Held back: Jeffrey D. Weaver - cannot sign`, `Marketing: Julie Pappas` or `Nobody named` - never more than 44 characters (`SHORT_CELL_MAX`, measured by clientcheck on the longest shapes the builders can produce), with the whole sentence in the new full-set column **`targetWhy`** and the resolved-domain note moved to the `website` cell as "(found by us)". A lead whose **Google listing points at one page inside a bigger site** rather than at a home page (`readOutletTell`: `/locations/kansas-city`, `/Raleigh`) is a **branch**: marked, kept as an email lead, and the **paid owner search is not bought** on it (`DM … the signer is at head office`) because three of them cost ten credits each and named nobody on 2026-09-04. The team page is now a real floor - it RAISES the band over their own words ("employs 5" against 23 names on their team page) but never over a verified headcount - the `🎯 TARGET` line no longer prints the marketing head's name after the word "owner", a lone "501(c)3" no longer drops a contractor who builds FOR one, and the Result line adds **"Of the N owner-run leads, M named (P%)"** so a branch is not counted as a missing owner.
- **Since [§118](../../../docs/history/round-118.md) (Vin: "we are usuing a lot of firecrawl credits lowkey" and "a company wioth a bad or poor wbesite") the website itself is scored, free, and the search that never worked is off.** The contact read now calls `readSiteBuild` on the markup it had ALREADY downloaded - no model call, no Firecrawl credit - plus `/robots.txt` and `/llms.txt` over plain HTTP. Ten faults in four groups: **converts** (no enquiry form anywhere we read, a phone that is not tappable), **build** (a build years out of date, a DIY-builder template), **GEO/AI** (no real business schema, a page painted by JavaScript so a crawler that does not run it sees almost nothing, a robots.txt shutting `GPTBot`/`ClaudeBot`/`PerplexityBot`/`CCBot`/`Google-Extended` out) and **SEO** (a noindex tag, a default title, no alt text). They add to a gap out of **25**, worded strong / fair / weak / poor, and that gap is the **eleventh FIT term** - level with `ads` and `founder`, below `size` (35). A good site scores **0 and is never penalised** (Vin's ruling); a site we could not read scores **null** and leaves the denominator, never 0. **We measure whether a site is BUILT to convert, never whether it DOES convert**: the row says "no form, no tap-to-call, no schema, DIY build" and never "your site converts badly". Log line `🖥 SITE`; the sheet gains a `site` cell inside the 44-character cap and a `siteWhy` column (contract 20261003). On the money: a Firecrawl **search costs 2 credits** and a **page read costs 1**, so 86% of the 2026-09-04 run went on owner searches and 14% on reading their websites. The **chamber-of-commerce query is off** (`DM_CHAMBER`) - 11 buys, 0 names, and it only ever fired on leads the trade query had already failed - gating it also stopped a duplicate model call on the same corpus, and the `💸 OWNER WAVE` line now names WHICH search was bought (`searches: owner_directory×3 …`) so the next cut is made on data.
- **Since [§119](../../../docs/history/round-119.md) (Vin: "these icp ratings are pretty low - is our grading off or are our signals off?") a broken website LIFTS a lead, a quiet one is no longer punished, and the email lane's count is the same population as its list.** The website gap left the scoring table after one round: as a 25-point term it lowered all 25 leads of the 2026-09-04 run and lifted none (74 with no site read, **65 with a clean one**), because in a ratio a term can only lift a lead above its own average by scoring above that average, and it needed a gap of 20 to break even when the worst site in the run was 13. It is a **lift on top of the score** now - strong 0, fair +2, weak +5, poor +8, **+40% for each sign the gap is bleeding** (ad code on their site, 150+ reviews), capped at +14 - so a good website costs a lead nothing at all. **No jobs page and no marketing tools stopped costing points**: together they were 26 of 220 on nearly every owner-run local business while the `ads` term paid 18 of 25 for the same silence. Hiring FOR marketing still scores the full 20. The **website grade out of 10** (10 best, 1 worst) sits in the column immediately right of the website; an unread site gets no grade. The **email lane was rolling over all along** - the lane counts, the export and the move button read one press while the cards under them read the whole queue, because the scope switch sat three bands lower and is sticky; it is one population now, with the switch on the row it governs. And the branch tell caught **Freeway Insurance** (13 credits, six paid searches, nobody named): a `locations.` subdomain is a tell on its own, a city slug matches on a hyphen boundary (`tampa` in `tampa-fl-33603`), and the host is read as the label before the public suffix instead of the first one.
- **Since [§120](../../../docs/history/round-120.md) the Email lane is the leads the rep never dials, and a lead measured on fewer than six signals is not scored at all.** The lane counts had gone identical (Call list (4), Email lane (4), the same four businesses) because a both-lane lead was in both lists; the email lane is email AND NOT call now, and the button carries the queue total beside the press (`Email lane (4 of 37)`). The website cell is a plain token - `poor`, not `poor (DIY build, no schema)` - with the grade beside it and the sentence in `siteWhy`. And **FIND_ICP_MIN_TERMS is six**: two leads whose site could not be read, with no owner and no address, scored 75/100 on four terms and outranked a lead with a named co-founder, a published mailbox and a phone. Raising it exposed a live defect it had been hiding - the `hiring` term built its sentence from an array the caller may not have set, and since findIcpScore records anything a term throws as NOT MEASURED, **a lead hiring for a marketing role silently left the score** whenever the titles list was missing.
- **Since [§121](../../../docs/history/round-121.md) (Vin: "i do not like that call volume per run has dropped") the resolver asks when it has nobody, a business with no website reaches the rep, and one lead cannot spend the day's budget.** Counted first: of the **five owner-run leads that press could work on, four were named - 80%, the target**. Call volume fell because half the press was shapes that can never be call leads. The one route left after the ladder fails - `findMarketingLeadViaHunter`, one Hunter credit and no Firecrawl - was gated on the business being LAYERED, so it stood down on exactly the owner-run leads where the ladder had just failed; it now runs whenever nobody is named, and `targetFor` no longer reports "nobody reachable" while holding a name and a title. A business with a phone and **no working website** goes on the call sheet ranked last. **A network blip is no longer an absence**: `transportFailure` tells a dropped connection from an answer at the one outbound door, `fetchTr` retries it once, `firecrawlSearch` carries "we could not ask" beside its empty array, and the 14-day owner negative memo refuses to remember a miss it never measured. **One lead may spend 10 credits** (`FIND_LEAD_CREDIT_CAP`); the size wave no longer fires when the owner wave already found nobody; and "stop reading once their pages name an owner" now applies where pages COST money, not only where they are free. An **UNKNOWN catch-all** no longer closes the Hunter finder and the other routes that never needed an SMTP verdict. The **BBB profile read is retired** (8 of 8 403s).
- **Since [§109](../../../docs/history/round-109.md):** the state-registry search is off unless `DM_REGISTRY=1` (0 for 11 on 2026-09-02); a first name that signs the Google review replies three or more times outranks a weak site title ("registered contractor") and ships as owner; "a member of" a trade body and a bare "tax-deductible" no longer drop a lead; a page heading ("Who We Are") can no longer be the decision-maker.
- **Nonprofit evidence on the pages** (a /donate page, "501(c)(3)", "tax-deductible", donation wording) DROPS the lead the same way, reason `nonprofit`; "we are not a nonprofit" never fires it. Institutions are one name list now (ministry, church, memory care, nursing home, hospice, school district), and "treatment center" is deliberately not on it because addiction treatment is a searched category ([§107](../../../docs/history/round-107.md)).
- **Chain evidence on the pages** (a "Franchise Owner" title; `/locations/<state>/<city>`; four or more state pages) was read before the paid wave and DROPPED the lead; "locally owned, not a franchise" never fires it ([§97](../../../docs/history/round-097.md), [§98](../../../docs/history/round-098.md)). Since [§114](../../../docs/history/round-114.md) only the franchise shapes drop; the location shapes mark a branch network, kept for email. Since [§115](../../../docs/history/round-115.md) the "<Brand> of <City>" territory on the brand's own domain is a franchise shape too, and a network is sized as its brand and treated as layered.
- **The owner ladder.** Stage 1, free: the team-page roster parser — a title must have an ownership word as its HEAD ("Partner Track" and "Meet the President" are not titles), professions count (Attorney, DDS, CPA), a first name alone ships marked, a CTA button or a company name in the name slot is refused, and a business named after its owner settles without spending ([§91](../../../docs/history/round-091.md), [§95](../../../docs/history/round-095.md), [§99](../../../docs/history/round-099.md), [§100](../../../docs/history/round-100.md), [§102](../../../docs/history/round-102.md), [§104](../../../docs/history/round-104.md)). Stage 1.5: whoever signs the Google review replies, via Apify, only when a place id exists ([§101](../../../docs/history/round-101.md)). Stage 2, paid: web search plus licence records, ~8–10 Firecrawl credits, default ON with a Settings switch, skipped when the lead has no city ([§95](../../../docs/history/round-095.md), [§104](../../../docs/history/round-104.md)). The free-settle rate is the whole cost lever (see `cost-model`).
- **Four owner grades** ride every row: confirmed, stated, inferred, unconfirmed — the name ships graded, with the sentence to open the call on, because a wrong name is one word the rep corrects on the phone. Since [§106](../../../docs/history/round-106.md) the sheet carries them as letters: **Owner grade** A (two sources agree), B (their own site says so), C (likely), D (held back); **Email grade** A (confirmed, or published as a person's), B (a real shared mailbox), C (a catch-all domain), D (a guess), with "(checker down)" when the verifier was out; the sentences are still one tick away in the full export ([§101](../../../docs/history/round-101.md)). A held-back name may be TESTED for an address but never ASSUMED: no email pattern is learned from it and a constructed guess is not sendable ([§96](../../../docs/history/round-096.md)).
- **Email**: tier 1 published on their site, tier 2 SMTP-confirmed, tier 3 learned pattern, tier 4 inferred (not sendable), tier 5 none; plus the states role mailbox (kept, marked — a front-desk box is still worth having), catch-all, and verifier-down ([§101](../../../docs/history/round-101.md)). One mailbox vocabulary decides junk / role / person ([§97](../../../docs/history/round-097.md)); a careers-page `recruiting@` is read last and scored down ([§97](../../../docs/history/round-097.md)); a page carrying none of the lead's own distinctive words flags a possible wrong company ([§100](../../../docs/history/round-100.md)). The verifier's free allowance is 100 checks a day and its latch re-tests itself after an outage ([§94](../../../docs/history/round-094.md), [§103](../../../docs/history/round-103.md)).
- **Phone** comes off the Google listing and is checked against their own site; a mismatch is a note about a tracking number, never a deletion ([§96](../../../docs/history/round-096.md)). **When to call** is read from their published hours against the trade ([§99](../../../docs/history/round-099.md); noon is no longer "12am", [§104](../../../docs/history/round-104.md)).
- **Three signals** off pages already read: ad code on the site (Google or Meta; "none found" only when the markup was readable), hiring for marketing (a TheirStack posting in hand WINS over an absent careers page, [§104](../../../docs/history/round-104.md)), and the published team size, which is a floor and says so ([§90](../../../docs/history/round-090.md)). A site already instrumented end to end (conversion tracking + call tracking + a real booking tool) is marked down, because somebody competent is already there ([§103](../../../docs/history/round-103.md)).
- **Three more signals since [§108](../../../docs/history/round-108.md)**, all from pages already read: **founder** (the owner named on their own pages, a "family-owned / founded by" phrase, the owner signing the review replies; a leadership page with two corporate titles scores zero), **scale** (an ESTIMATED size band from a verified or published headcount, a fleet, a location count or a long tenure — always worded "estimated", never a figure, never in an email; over the $30M ceiling marks the lead down, under the $800k floor benches it — [§111](../../../docs/history/round-111.md)), and **invests** (one or two marketing tools is the best prospect shape). Hiring reads field roles and recency; affordability reads financing, commercial clients and 15+ years. Ownership tells on the page ("a division of", "portfolio company", "serving nationwide", the franchisee disclosure beside the word franchise) dropped the lead with reason `owned` / `franchise` / `national`; since [§114](../../../docs/history/round-114.md) only `franchise` drops, and `owned` / `national` mark the lead PE-owned / national and route it to email.
- **The FIT score** is scored only after the lookups, over at least three measured signals; under three it says "not scored" and sorts last, because a ratio over one term is not a fit score ([§98](../../../docs/history/round-098.md), [§105](../../../docs/history/round-105.md)).
- Every read is stamped with the build that made it, and a read from an older parser is flagged, never silently re-bought ([§97](../../../docs/history/round-097.md)).

- **Since [§122](../../../docs/history/round-122.md) (Vin: "I just run a bunch then dump them into excel, rinse and repeat") the contact panel opens with the RUN, not the queue, and a read lead can no longer be un-read.** The panel rendered **45 numbers** over **two populations** — the tabs and the unread counts read the whole queue, the lanes, the CSV and the Result line read a *scope* that defaulted to the queue and was persisted, with its switch three bands below the numbers it moved. `Call list (15 of 336)` was the panel printing both because it could not choose, and it read as a fraction. Now: the last press is the view (recovered after a refresh from the `contactRunId` on each lead), there is no scope toggle, every `of N` is gone, a **zero is not drawn**, and the buttons name their cargo — **`Download these 15`**, which cannot mean the queue. One number leads it, `ready to call` = `exportableContact`, and it is the SAME number during the run, when it ends, on the button and in the file: the loading bar used to count `withOwner` ("the ladder found a name") which is why it said 17 and the CSV said 15. The panel says whether those rows are **already in the spreadsheet** (`all 15 are new` / `12 new, 3 you already downloaded`) from the `exportedAt` stamp, and **`N never exported`** is the one way out of the run view — without it a second press before an export buries the first run. The thirty-number Result sentence is not deleted: it is one click away under **`what happened in this run`**, beside the number it explains. **8 numbers on the panel, 3 in the block he looks at.** Three defects went with it: the Supabase restore let the cloud row win every name collision, so a row pushed BEFORE a read (or by a FAILED read) put a paid lead back in the queue and erased its export stamp — `read` is monotonic now, both directions; `saveDiscovered` DELETEd and re-POSTed the whole durable queue **once per lead** from a pool of six, leaving it momentarily empty each time — the DELETE is gone (the POST was always an upsert) and the push is debounced to one write a batch; and the queue cap kept the HEAD of a list a Find press PREPENDS to, so the rows it binned were the oldest — the ones we had paid ~4.7 credits each to read. `capQueue` ranks instead: an un-exported call lead last of all, then any read lead, then a verdict, then unread.

## 3. The four tabs

**Not read** (never read, or the read FAILED — a dead server might work next time, so it comes back), **Read**, **Ruled out** (a server VERDICT: asking again cannot change it; "Put them back" exists), **Nothing to read** (nothing left; offers Find more and the other lanes). Every control that spends lives on one tab ([§99](../../../docs/history/round-099.md), [§105](../../../docs/history/round-105.md)). A tab whose count is **zero is not drawn**, and neither is an empty lane, except the one you are standing on ([§122](../../../docs/history/round-122.md)). A tick box per card plus "Move the N you just read" sends them to Research ([§97](../../../docs/history/round-097.md)). The view choices survive a refresh — but **the run/queue scope does not, and no longer exists**: since [§122](../../../docs/history/round-122.md) the panel always shows the last press, because Vin exports per run and keeps his record in Excel. It was persisted and defaulted to the queue, which meant a download button could silently hand over 380 rows instead of the 15 just read ([§97](../../../docs/history/round-097.md), [§122](../../../docs/history/round-122.md)).
